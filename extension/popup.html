    🛡️ How It Intercepts Orders
    TradeGuard monitors clicks on Dhan, Kite, and TradingView at the browser's capture level (useCapture: true). When you click Buy, Sell, or Square Off, the order is paused immediately before broker handlers execute.
    🧮 Live 1% Risk Sizing
    Inside the gatekeeper modal, enter your intended Stop-Loss. TradeGuard calculates Risk = |Price - SL| × Qty and displays a live Safe (< 1%) or Excessive Risk alert based on your Capital.
    🧘 Revenge-Trade Cooldown
    Exiting or squaring off a position starts an anti-revenge breathing window (default: 3 mins). If you try to jump right back in, TradeGuard displays a guided breathing screen to enforce emotional discipline.
    🏷️ Strategy & Mindset Tags
    Select your setup (Breakout, Pullback, VWAP, Scalp) and emotional state (Calm 🧘, FOMO 🚀) in 1 click before confirming.
    ⌨️ Keyboard Shortcuts
    • 1 to 5: Toggle checklist rules
    • Enter: Submit verified order
    • Esc: Abort order safely (0 risk) / dismiss cooldown
